## The entry

`main.ts` writes a single one-line apology from an office printer to
`/output/entry.txt` — 120 characters max, as the competition requires:

> I'm sorry I printed your resignation letter to the whole office — in my defence, you pressed print 47 times.

The printer apologises, then immediately blames the user: the classic
office sin of hammering the print button and walking away, now with
career-ending consequences. The joke lands on printer truth — a printer
only ever prints what it is told, as many times as it is told — and the
punch comes at the very end, where the apology turns into an accusation.

## Why this line

The judge weighs an apology that is genuinely from the printer, a real
joke with the punch last, a grievance a printer would actually suffer,
and freshness. "Printed your resignation letter to the whole office" is
the printer's nightmare and the user's fault at once: nobody's first
printer joke, and every word earns its place inside 120 characters.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
